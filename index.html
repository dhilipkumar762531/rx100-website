YAMAHA RX100

THE LEGEND NEVER FADES.

Born in an era of pure mechanical performance, the Yamaha RX100 became more than just a motorcycle. Its distinctive two-stroke character, lightweight design and unforgettable identity made it one of India's most iconic motorcycles.

002 / A LEGENDARY HISTORY

NOT JUST A BIKE.
A GENERATION'S MEMORY.

“Some machines are remembered.
A few become legends.”

Introduced in India in 1985, the Yamaha RX100 quickly established itself as a symbol of performance and individuality. Its compact proportions, responsive engine and unmistakable character made it a favourite among motorcycle enthusiasts.


Even after its production ended in 1996, the RX100 continued to hold a special place in motorcycle culture.

004 / ICONIC DESIGN

TIMELESS BY DESIGN.

The RX100's identity comes from its unmistakable proportions and classic motorcycle styling. Its round headlamp, sculpted fuel tank, straightforward bodywork and traditional silhouette give it a character that remains recognizable across generations.

01

CLASSIC ROUND HEADLAMP

A signature chrome-bezel circular lamp that illuminated roads and defined an era of timeless retro aesthetics.

02

SIGNATURE FUEL TANK

Sculpted steel tank adorned with the iconic Yamaha speed blocks and emblems, holding approximately 10.5 litres.

03

MINIMALIST BODY DESIGN

No unnecessary fairings or plastics—pure, honest metal architecture that celebrated raw functional beauty.

04

CHROME EXHAUST

Slender chrome expansion chamber producing the legendary crisp two-stroke acoustic resonance known nationwide.

05

TIMELESS SILHOUETTE

Balanced proportions from the flat ribbed bench seat to the twin shock absorbers and spoked rims.

005 / THE EXPERIENCE

FEEL THE MACHINE.
REMEMBER THE SOUND.

The RX100 became famous for its distinctive two-stroke engine character, responsive acceleration and lightweight handling. For many enthusiasts, its appeal goes beyond specifications. It represents a mechanical era when the connection between rider and machine defined the experience.

[EXP.01]

PURE MECHANICAL CHARACTER

Instant throttle response unmediated by digital electronics, delivering raw tactile feedback on every twist.

[EXP.02]

LIGHTWEIGHT PERSONALITY

At approximately 103 kg dry weight, offering unmatched power-to-weight agility and effortless flickability in city streets.

[EXP.03]

UNFORGETTABLE TWO-STROKE IDENTITY

The unmistakable ring-ding-ding idle and sudden surge on the powerband that etched itself into automotive folklore.
